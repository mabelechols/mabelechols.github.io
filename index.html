About Me

Welcome!!

I am Mabel Echols (she/her). I am a scientist, engineer, programmer, and artist.

Currently, I am a first-year master's student at Northeastern University, studying Robotics with a concentration in Mechanical Engineering. My undergraduate degree is in Materials Science and Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University. My technical interests include mechanism design, advanced manufacturing techniques, and polymers.

I have worked on a variety of technical projects, including industry projects, academic research, course projects, and self-led personal technical projects. My projects page has a selection of my projects on it, and when possible, I have included technical documentation of my processes for reference. If you have any questions or are interested in chatting, please feel free to shoot me an email.
