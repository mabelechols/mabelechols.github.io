Modular Pill Dispenser

As part of my Master's program at Northeastern University, I took a course called Assistive Robotics, focused on assistive device design, human machine interaction, and needs evaluation. For my course project I designed and prototyped a modular pill dispenser which would automatically release the correct amount of medication at the correct time with a small group of other students. The modules were easily tilable and an arbitrary number of different shaped pills were supported, with a graphical user interface allowing for easy schedule setting!
